Small steps. Big adventures.

Give your puppy the foundations they need to grow into a confident, connected and capable dog.

For puppies aged 3–5 months

Start your puppy's journey the right way

Puppyhood is a time of enormous learning. This course gives you the knowledge and practical skills to understand how your puppy learns, build great habits and make training part of everyday life. We focus on positive, practical training that helps puppies and their people learn together.

What you'll learn

We combine explanation, practical training and real-life distractions so that you understand not just what to teach, but how and why it works.

  • Luring, shaping and capturing behaviours
  • How dogs learn and the mechanics of teaching behaviours
  • Motivation and troubleshooting during training
  • Leash manners and everyday life skills
  • Basic obedience foundations
  • Training around everyday distractions
  • Positive socialisation opportunities
  • Recall and introductory nose work
  • Handling and confidence-building exercises
  • Building attention and engagement with you

More than just puppy training

The goal isn't a puppy who can perform a list of behaviours. It's a puppy who can learn, communicate and feel confident as they discover the world with you.

Confidence

Help your puppy develop positive experiences with handling, learning and the world around them.

Connection

Build attention, engagement and cooperation so that your puppy learns that working with you is rewarding.

Real-life skills

Practise useful foundations such as walking nicely on lead, recall and coping with everyday distractions.

Understanding

Learn to recognise how your puppy learns and how to adjust your training when something isn't working.

The Puppy Course
