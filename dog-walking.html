Personalised care

Walks that work for your dog

Not every dog wants the same kind of walk. Some love a long adventure, some prefer time to sniff and explore, and some benefit from a calmer, more predictable outing.

Hazel Tails offers individual walks, giving your dog one-to-one attention rather than being part of a large group. Walks can be adapted to your dog's age, energy level, confidence and usual routine.

Calm. Individual. Positive.

Your dog gets dedicated attention from the same familiar person, with their wellbeing and enjoyment at the centre of every walk.

Good to know

Dog walking FAQs

Do you walk dogs individually?

Yes. Hazel Tails dog walks are designed as individual walks, allowing the outing to be tailored to your dog rather than managing a large group.

What areas do you cover?

Dog walking is currently focused around Castelldefels and nearby areas. Your exact location will be confirmed when you enquire.

Can you walk puppies?

Puppy walks can be considered depending on your puppy's age, vaccination status, routine and individual needs. We'll discuss this during the initial enquiry.

Can you reinforce training during the walk?

Everyday skills such as attention and calm lead walking can be naturally reinforced where appropriate. If you are looking for dedicated behavioural or training work, a Private Class is a better option.

What happens if my dog is nervous or doesn't like other dogs?

This is something we'll discuss before accepting a booking. Individual walks can be suitable for dogs who don't enjoy group walks, but your dog's safety and wellbeing will always come first.

What happens if it is extremely hot or bad weather?

Your dog's welfare comes first. Walks may be shortened, rescheduled or adapted when weather conditions make a normal walk unsuitable. This is particularly important during periods of high heat.
