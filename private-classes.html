Private Classes

Your dog. Your goals. Your training plan.

Private training gives you the opportunity to work one-to-one with Ella, focusing on the behaviours and skills that matter most to you and your dog.

Sessions are tailored to your dog's individual needs rather than following a one-size-fits-all programme. Whether you're bringing home a new puppy, navigating adolescence or looking for support with an adult dog, we'll work together to find practical solutions that fit your everyday life.
