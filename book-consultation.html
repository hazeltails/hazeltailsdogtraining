A personalised approach

Training starts with understanding

Whether you have a brand-new puppy, a teenage tornado, or an older dog who needs a little extra support, a consultation is the first step towards creating a training plan that works for both of you.

We'll talk about what's happening at home, what you'd like to change, and what you'd love to achieve with your dog. There is no judgement and no one-size-fits-all approach — just practical, positive training designed around you and your dog.

What to expect

Your consultation

A relaxed conversation where we can get to know each other and work out how I can best support you.

1

Tell me about your dog

We'll talk about your dog's personality, history, current behaviour and anything you're finding challenging.

2

Talk about your goals

What would you like life with your dog to look like? We'll identify the things that matter most to you.

3

Create a plan

Together we'll work out the most appropriate next steps and the kind of training that will best suit you and your dog.
