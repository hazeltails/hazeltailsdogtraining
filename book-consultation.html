What should I include?

Don't worry about writing the perfect email. A few simple details are enough to get the conversation started.

🐾 About your dog

Their name, age, breed or mix, and anything else you think would be useful to know.

💬 What you'd like help with

Tell me what you're currently struggling with or what you'd like your dog to learn.

🏡 Your everyday life

A little about your home, routine and the situations where you'd most like things to improve.

📅 Your availability

Let me know roughly when you're available for training and I'll do my best to find a suitable time.
