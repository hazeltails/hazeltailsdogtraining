About Hazel Tails Dog Training

My name is Ella and I am from England. Dogs have always been a big part of my family life, and I grew up surrounded by them as part of our family pets. I have always been driven by a deep curiosity about how animals think and learn, especially dogs. This curiosity inspired me to undertake a biology degree, which later led into a career teaching secondary school science.

In 2020 I moved to Spain/Castelldefels, and in 2024 I adopted my Labrador, Kili, and began training with Isabelle through Green Dog Training. Working closely with Kili deepened my understanding of everyday dog behaviour and communication, and inspired me to follow my passion professionally. He continues to be a big part of my learning an we love training together.

In 2026 I completed the Green Dog Training's Dog Trainer course, where I developed the skills to combine my scientific background with my teaching experience to help owners better understand their dogs and build stronger relationships with them.I believe that training should always be a positive experience for both dog and owner, strengthening trust, understanding, and connection.

I set up Hazel Tails Dog Training as an affiliate of Green Dog Training, where I focus on my mission to give puppies the best possible start in life, to offer a reliable and dependable service providing consistency for you and your dog, and to do what I can to reduce the number of dogs that end up in shelters.
