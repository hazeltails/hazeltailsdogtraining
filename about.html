Ella and Kili

My journey into dog training

My name is Ella and I am from England. Dogs have always been a big part of my family life, and I grew up surrounded by them as part of our family pets. I have always been driven by a deep curiosity about how animals think and learn, especially dogs. This curiosity inspired me to undertake a biology degree, which later led into a career teaching secondary school science.

In 2020, I moved to Spain and settled in Castelldefels. In 2024, I adopted my Labrador, Kili, and began training with Isabelle through Green Dog Training. Working closely with Kili deepened my understanding of everyday dog behaviour and communication, and inspired me to follow my passion professionally. He continues to be a big part of my learning, and we love training together.

In 2026, I completed the Green Dog Training Dog Trainer Course, where I developed the skills to combine my scientific background with my teaching experience to help owners better understand their dogs and build stronger relationships with them.

I believe that training should always be a positive experience for both dog and owner, strengthening trust, understanding and connection.

Why Hazel Tails?

Training with a bigger purpose

I set up Hazel Tails Dog Training as an affiliate of Green Dog Training, with a focus on giving puppies the best possible start in life and helping dogs and their owners build a strong foundation together.

I want to provide a reliable and dependable service that gives you and your dog consistency and support throughout your training journey.

Most importantly, I want to do what I can to help reduce the number of dogs that end up in shelters by helping owners understand their dogs, build strong relationships with them and feel confident navigating everyday life together.

The Hazel Tails Approach
