Hazel Tails Training Courses

Building skills for every stage of growing up.

Our courses are designed to support you and your dog through different stages of development, from those first important puppy experiences to the practical skills needed for everyday life.

Find your course

Where are you and your dog starting from?

Choose the course that best matches your dog's age and stage. Both courses use positive, practical training to help you build confidence, communication and a strong relationship with your dog.

Puppies aged 3–5 months

Puppy Course

Small steps. Big adventures.

Give your puppy a positive start as they learn about you, the world around them and the skills that will help them grow into a confident family dog.

The Puppy Course focuses on building strong foundations through positive experiences, practical training and helping you understand what your puppy is communicating.

The course can help with:

  • Building focus and engagement with you
  • Introducing useful everyday skills
  • Developing confidence in new situations
  • Positive socialisation experiences
  • Starting recall and lead skills
  • Understanding puppy behaviour and communication

Dogs aged 6 months+

Life Skills Course

Skills for life beyond puppyhood.

As puppies grow, the world can suddenly become much more exciting. The Life Skills Course helps you continue building the behaviours and communication you need for everyday life.

Designed for dogs from six months onwards, the course focuses on practical skills that help you and your dog navigate walks, distractions and everyday situations together.

The course can help with:

  • Focus around distractions
  • Loose-lead walking
  • Building a stronger recall
  • Settling and calm behaviour
  • Impulse control and good choices
  • Strengthening communication with your dog
