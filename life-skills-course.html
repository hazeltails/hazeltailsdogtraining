Skills for real life

Training isn't just about teaching your dog behaviours. It's about helping them navigate the world with confidence while building a stronger relationship with you.

The Life Skills Course is a fun, interactive five-week course for dogs aged 6 months and over. You'll practise practical skills in a group environment, learn how to work around distractions and develop tools that make everyday life easier for both ends of the lead.

Is this course right for you?

The course is suitable for dogs at all stages of adulthood and for handlers at all levels of experience.

Your dog is 6 months or older

There is no maximum age. Whether your dog is young, mature or enjoying their later years, there is always more to learn.

You want useful everyday skills

Work on focus, lead manners, impulse control, settling and other skills that can make everyday situations easier.

You enjoy learning together

Expect practical exercises, group activities, games and plenty of opportunities to develop your training skills.

What we'll cover

The course focuses on practical skills that can be shaped, improved and practised around real-life distractions.

  • Focus on your handler around other dogs
  • Leash manners
  • Impulse control
  • Stay and settle
  • Terrace training
  • Emergency stop
  • Circuit training and confidence building
  • Trick training
  • Luring and capturing behaviours
  • Fun group exercises inspired by everyday situations

Learning together

Practising around other dogs and people gives you opportunities to build your skills beyond the quiet environment of your living room.

A positive group experience

This course is designed for dogs who are comfortable working in a group environment with other dogs and people. If your dog is showing aggressive or reactive behaviour towards dogs or humans, private training may be a more appropriate starting point.

All Hazel Tails group courses are conducted in English.
